Privacy policy

This Google OAuth client is used by a single person, the owner of the Google account it accesses, through the open-source tool rclone, to copy that person's own files between their own computers and their own Google Drive.

  • Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only for that file syncing.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared with anyone. Files move only between the owner's own computers and the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 14 November 2026.
